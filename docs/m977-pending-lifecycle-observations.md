trace. Preparation-phase points are discarded; the ready draft and its history
are empty. The distinct four ready points produce a real selected candidate,
archived part and ready preview. Cancel preserves the complete raw project.

countryLabels. This is not proof of a full-application load or product defect.
No fixture, baseline normalizer or pinned web source is changed to hide it.

The exact established model-exchange document history scope is retained:
territorialEntities, sourceInfo, labels, genericFeatures, hydroEdits, timelineRecords,
geometries, distributionLayers and distributionEntries. All structural values,
array order, numerical values and references restore exactly. Each full raw save
and the seven precise raw history deltas remain in the evidence. Unexpected
presentation/value/order changes are rejected, rather than covered by a blanket
normalization rule.

The new runtime also reuses production spatial invalidation and Worker-sync wiring.
Thus revision changes and Undo/Redo synchronization are observed through production
code, not fabricated fixture counters. Full web startup remains outside this slice.
